Season 1 of the HCA is Here
The Hockey Club Association officially launches with 18 franchises across two conferences. All games will be played CPU vs CPU in NHL26 and streamed live on Twitch at tml_ttv. Rosters have been set, lines are locked in — it's time to play hockey.

How the HCA Works
Each of the 18 franchises is owned and managed by a real GM. Trades, signings, and line changes are submitted to the commissioner group and applied in-game. Games are streamed live on Twitch for the whole world to watch.
